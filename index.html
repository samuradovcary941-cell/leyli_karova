Art that remembers its roots.

Leyli Karova is a Turkmen artist who paints ornament and portraits by hand on blazers. Traditional jewellery, headdresses and carpet patterns become clothing meant to be worn, not stored. The motif can be Turkmen or any other: the story is yours to choose.

Each piece is made for one person and is never repeated.

How a piece is made

One person, one blazer, four steps.
  1. 01

    Choose the ornament

    You pick the motif, its meaning and where it sits on the blazer. You also choose how the jewellery is made: stones painted by hand, or real fittings sewn on.

  2. 02

    Sketch

    The pattern is drawn to the cut of your blazer before any paint touches it.

  3. 03

    Paint by hand

    Each line is painted by Leyli. No prints, no copies.

  4. 04

    Receive your piece

    The finished blazer arrives as a one-of-one work.
